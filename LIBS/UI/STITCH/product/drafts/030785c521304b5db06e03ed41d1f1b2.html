Help & Offline Field Manual

Actionable guidance stored locally in reader firmware

menu_book 6 Guides Available
download_for_offline Getting a knowledge pack (.zim)
expand_more

ZIM files contain complete offline websites, encyclopedias, and medical repositories. To acquire a pack:

  1. Download official packages from Kiwix or open mirrors using a stable connection.
  2. Transfer the `.zim` file to internal storage or your secondary SD card.
  3. Tap Library > Open a ZIM in the left rail to index articles instantly without decompression.
pie_chart Managing storage budget
expand_more

Large video or media-rich encyclopedias can exceed 50GB. To conserve device space:

  • Select "nopic" versions (text-only editions) for high-density reference with 90% less disk footprint.
  • Mount storage directly on external SD cards formatted as exFAT.
  • Clear auxiliary search indices from unlinked archives in Settings > Storage.
find_in_page Reader navigation & search
expand_more

Full-text offline search uses Xapian pre-indexed tables:

  • Press Ctrl+K or / anywhere to trigger rapid omni-search.
  • Use prefix queries like title:mitochondria for instant header targeting.
  • Browse article table of contents via the floating drawer on right desktop margins.
edit_note Working with annotations & notes
expand_more

Highlight passages and write markdown reflections right alongside reference articles:

  • Select text within any article canvas to trigger the 4-color quick-highlight bar.
  • Notes are decoupled from the read-only ZIM binary and stored in your device workspace.
  • Review all annotations aggregated by topic under the Notes tab in the rail.
devices Sharing files across devices
expand_more

Distribute knowledge packs without internet access in field expeditions:

  • Use USB-OTG flash drives to physically share `.zim` archives between Android, Linux, and Windows.
  • Peer-to-peer Wi-Fi hotspot mode allows local readers to browse shared ZIM files hosted on your machine.
restore_page Backup & recovery
expand_more

Ensure your research notes and highlight collections survive system upgrades:

  • Export regular `.enzbak` archives from the "Your Data" section above.
  • During restore operations, EnZIME displays an exact conflict inspection screen before writing data.
  • Passphrase protected backups use industry standard Argon2id key derivation.
