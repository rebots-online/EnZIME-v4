download_for_offline Getting a knowledge pack
expand_more

Open Library, then Available downloads. Choose a pack and Plan download. If you already have a ZIM file, choose Open a ZIM.

pie_chart Managing storage budget
expand_more

In the download plan, choose your preferred size. Spread cuts evenly or prioritize a domain, then compute the plan before starting. Large files are shared across your apps.

find_in_page Reader navigation & search
expand_more

Open an installed pack. Use Contents or Search this pack to find an article. Switch between Reader and Navigator without losing your place.

edit_note Working with annotations & notes
expand_more

Select a passage and choose Highlight or Note. Use Text, Ink, Voice, Photo or Video to add your own work. Find saved annotations in Notes.

devices Sharing files across devices
expand_more

In Notes, choose Export for the selected notes or notebook. On another device, choose Import and review the file before adding it.

restore_page Backup & recovery
expand_more

In Your data, choose Export backup. To restore, choose a backup file and review its contents and conflicts before confirming.
